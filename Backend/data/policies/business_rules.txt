Insurance Self-Service Portal Business Rules

Policy Lookup
Users must provide a valid policy number to access their policy information.
If the policy number does not exist, the system will return a "Policy not found" message.

Authentication
Users may only access policies that belong to them.
The system must verify the user's identity before displaying sensitive policy details.

Policy Status
Policies may have one of the following statuses:
- Active
- Lapsed
- Cancelled
- Pending

Only active policies allow claims to be submitted.

Premium Payments
Premiums are billed monthly.
Payments must be made on or before the due date.

Grace Period
A grace period of 30 days is allowed for late premium payments.
If payment is not received within the grace period, the policy status will change to "Lapsed".

Policy Reinstatement
Lapsed policies may be reinstated within 90 days from the lapse date.
All outstanding premiums must be paid before reinstatement can occur.

Beneficiary Updates
Policyholders may update beneficiaries through the self-service portal.
Changes to beneficiaries will only take effect after successful verification.

Claims Submission
Claims may only be submitted for active policies.
The system must verify that the policy meets waiting period requirements before allowing claim submission.

Claim Processing
Once all required documents are submitted, claims are processed within 5–10 business days.

System Notifications
The system will notify users when:
- A premium payment is due
- A policy has lapsed
- A claim has been approved or rejected

Data Privacy
Policyholder data must be protected and only displayed to authorized users.
The system must comply with data protection regulations.
