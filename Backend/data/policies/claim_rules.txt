Claims Rules for Funeral Policies

Claim Eligibility
A claim may only be submitted for an active policy.
The policy must be up to date with premium payments.

Waiting Period
Natural death claims are subject to a waiting period of 6 months from the policy start date.
If the policyholder dies within the waiting period due to natural causes, the claim will be rejected.

Accidental Death Claims
Accidental death is covered immediately from the start date of the policy.
The beneficiary may submit a claim if the policyholder dies due to an accident.

Required Documents
To process a funeral claim, the following documents must be submitted:
- A certified copy of the death certificate
- A completed claim form
- A certified copy of the policyholder's identification document
- A certified copy of the beneficiary's identification document

Additional documents may be requested if required for verification.

Claim Submission
Claims may be submitted through the self-service portal or at an authorized branch.

Claim Verification
All submitted documents will be verified by the claims department.
If the documents are incomplete or incorrect, the claim will be placed on hold until the correct documents are provided.

Claim Processing Time
Once all required documents are received and verified, claims will be processed within 5 to 10 business days.

Claim Payout
Approved claims will be paid to the registered beneficiary listed on the policy.

Claim Rejection
A claim may be rejected under the following circumstances:
- The policy is not active
- Required documents are missing
- The waiting period has not been satisfied for natural death
- Fraud or misrepresentation is detected
