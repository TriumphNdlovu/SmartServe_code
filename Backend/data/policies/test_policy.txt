Funeral Policy Rules

Eligibility
The policyholder must be at least 18 years old and not older than 65 years at the time of application.
Dependents covered under the policy must be declared during the application process.

Waiting Period
Natural death claims have a waiting period of 6 months.

If the policyholder dies within 6 months of starting the policy,
the claim will be rejected unless the death was accidental.

Accidental Death
Accidental death is covered immediately from the start date of the policy.
The beneficiary may submit a claim if the policyholder dies due to an accident at any time after the policy becomes active.

Premium Payments
Premiums must be paid monthly.
If the premium is not paid for 30 days after the due date, the policy may lapse.

Policy Reinstatement
A lapsed policy may be reinstated within 90 days of the lapse date,
provided all outstanding premiums are paid.

Beneficiaries
The policyholder must nominate at least one beneficiary.
Benefits will be paid to the registered beneficiary upon a valid claim.

Claims Process
To submit a funeral claim, the beneficiary must provide:
- A certified copy of the death certificate
- A completed claim form
- Identification documents of the policyholder and beneficiary

Claims will be processed within 5 to 10 working days after all required documents are received.

Coverage
The policy provides funeral cover for:
- The policyholder
- The spouse
- Dependent children listed on the policy

The payout amount will depend on the coverage level selected by the policyholder.
